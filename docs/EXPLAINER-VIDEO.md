# Explainer video: play, bank, build

## Goal

Show the complete offline LoD loop: play several characters and find gear, move chosen items into one shared bank, search/filter and send useful gear to another character, plan a runeword, then make a skill/stat respec. Keep the story grounded in actual app and game footage.

## Target cut

- 75–95 seconds, 16:9, 1080p, captions always on.
- Diablo palette, restrained ember transitions, no busy motion over the app.
- Voiceover can be recorded after the picture cut; captions remain readable without audio.
- Use a temporary copy of character and stash saves for every app transfer or save edit. Never show real filesystem paths, desktop notifications, account information, or unrelated apps.

## Script

The full timed voiceover and shot-by-shot script is in [EXPLAINER-VIDEO-SCRIPT.md](./EXPLAINER-VIDEO-SCRIPT.md). This planning file keeps the capture and production notes.

## Capture notes

- Gameplay clips must be real D2 LoD footage from the user's session; do not fabricate in-game UI or imply that the app edits Battle.net characters.
- Capture at 1920×1080 landscape when possible. Record clean clips without voice; we will add captions and mix narration/music in editing.
- App capture uses a separate preview profile and disposable copies of the provided characters/stash. Keep a pristine copy for each take because deposits and respec saves are real edits to the selected demo file.
- For bank transfer proof, show an item disappear from the source grid and appear as an available bank card. For export proof, show the item appear in the chosen character destination. Avoid a copy-only visual that could suggest duplication.
- Do not show Battle.net online play or claim server-side duplicate protection. The current app is an offline LoD tool.
- Do not stage a Cube-to-runeword interaction. The Cube can upgrade runes; to create the runeword, socket the ordered runes into the correct nonmagical base in-game.
