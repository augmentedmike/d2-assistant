# D2 Assistant explainer video script

## Production brief

- **Runtime:** about 90 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9, 1080p, captions always on
- **Voice:** first person, conversational; record after picture edit
- **Music:** restrained dark-fantasy instrumental, kept below narration
- **Footage:** real offline Diablo II: Lord of Destruction gameplay and D2 Assistant screens. Use disposable copies of saves for app demonstrations.

## Timed script and shots

| Time | Picture and edit direction | Voiceover / captions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10s | Cold open on real LoD gameplay. Cut between two or three characters; show one pick up an item. Title: **One offline D2 workflow**. | “After years of playing Diablo II on Mac, I got tired of bouncing between separate tools to move gear, edit characters, and look up recipes. I wanted one offline assistant that keeps the whole loop together.” |
| 10–23s | Show a character’s inventory or private stash grid. Select a few items, move them into the shared bank, then show those items removed from the source and available in the bank. | “When I find gear, I select the items in a character’s inventory or private stash and move them into the shared bank. They leave that save and become available in the bank.” |
| 23–37s | Show bank cards. Search `tir`, `tal`, or `eth`; demonstrate one type or level filter. Select a rune and a piece of equipment, choose a different character, and transfer to inventory or stash. | “Search short rune names like Tir, Tal, or Eth. Filter by type, level, sockets, and more. Then choose exactly what another character needs and send it to their inventory or stash.” |
| 37–47s | Cut back to gameplay. Open the destination character’s stash, then show the transferred equipment. | “No more juggling separate stash and editor tools just to get the right gear onto the right character.” |
| 47–62s | Open **Runeword Recipes**. Search **Spirit**. Hold on rune order, socket count, valid base types, and ruleset availability. Select the ingredients in the bank and transfer them. | “Before I commit runes, I check the recipe: the exact order, socket count, valid bases, and supported game rulesets.” |
| 62–74s | In game, show the correct nonmagical socketed base. If using the Cube, show a genuine rune upgrade as a separate action. Socket the listed runes in order; show the completed item and equip it. | “If I need a higher rune, I can upgrade it in the Cube. Then I socket the runes in order in game and equip the finished runeword.” |
| 74–85s | Show **Skills & stats**. Show available points, reset, and a valid reallocation. Include the backup/save confirmation. | “When I want to try another build, I can reset skills and attributes, then spend only the points the character has earned. A backup is saved before changes are applied.” |
| 85–92s | Return to gameplay with the equipped item. End card with logo, repository link, and **Offline Diablo II assistant**. | “D2 Assistant brings offline item management, character planning, and runeword recipes together in one place.” |

## On-screen text

Keep captions synchronized to the voiceover. Use short labels only where they clarify a feature:

- **Select → Bank → Transfer**
- **Search runes and gear**
- **Check order, sockets, base, and ruleset**
- **Respec with earned points**
- End card: **D2 Assistant · Offline Diablo II companion**

## Capture and accuracy notes

- Do not record or use gameplay footage until Michael is ready and explicitly starts the capture session. The script is prepared without capturing anything.
- Use real LoD footage. Do not imply Battle.net or online character editing, server-side duplicate checks, or D2R support that has not been verified.
- For app transfers and respec, use a separate preview profile and disposable copies of character and stash saves. Confirm each transfer visually: the item leaves its source and appears in its destination.
- Runewords are made by socketing the runes into the correct nonmagical base in the listed order. The Cube upgrades runes; it does not create runewords.
- Hide filesystem paths, account details, notifications, and unrelated windows. Capture clean footage without voice; add captions and narration in the edit.
- The video stays a draft until Michael reviews the completed cut.
